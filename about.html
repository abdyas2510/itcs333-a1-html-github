About Me

I study network engineering at the University of Bahrain. Outside of coursework, most of what I build ends up being some version of "make two systems that were never supposed to talk to each other, talk to each other." Lately that's meant car head units, mostly, or whatever IoT sensor's lying around.

This semester

  1. Network Security I, which is basically a semester of professionally organized paranoia
  2. IoT: hooking sensors and small devices up to the internet, then dealing with the fact that they don't want to cooperate
  3. AI, the actual search-and-reasoning kind, not the chatbot kind
  4. Circuit Analysis, the class that keeps reminding me networks are still, annoyingly, hardware
